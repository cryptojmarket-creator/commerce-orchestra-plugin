Commerce Orchestra is an early pilot that helps prepare actions on third-party commerce services. Availability, prices, inventory, delivery estimates, and platform behavior remain controlled by those services.

The user must review the selected item, quantity, options, merchant, delivery details, and total before approving payment. The pilot does not authorize unattended payment.

The plugin may use an existing account entitlement established independently outside Codex. It does not display plans, initiate a subscription, or create a payment link. Account management and any payment approval occur outside the Codex plugin.
